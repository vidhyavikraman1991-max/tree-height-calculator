3. Calculation Steps

How does the formula work?

In a right-angled triangle, tangent is the ratio of the opposite side to the adjacent side.

tan(α) = h / d

Rearranging gives:

h = d × tan(α)

This calculates the height from eye level to the top of the tree. Add the observer's eye height to estimate the total tree height.
